Optional. Awards may not exceed USD 1,000 per awardee (maximum five awards each). See the full award criteria before applying.

Award application details

The applicant MUST be the presenting author. No exceptions will be made.

You must be a member of the society whose award you apply for — please confirm this in the membership question above. If you are not yet a member, the application must be initiated prior to the abstract submission closure date and accepted for your award application to be considered. TTS membership: tts.org/member-area/member

Signed by your Training Program Director to verify your status as a trainee/young investigator — or, for emerging economy applicants, proof of residency.

Authors will not be considered for an award if the required letter is not sent.

Do not register yet. Award recipients receive complimentary registration. If you are applying for an award, please wait until notifications are sent in mid-January 2027 — recipients will be sent a dedicated registration link and will not be charged. Early-bird rates remain available until February 15, 2027.
4 · Declarations
What happens next. You will receive an email confirmation once your submission is received. Abstracts may be edited until the deadline by emailing program@isvca2027.org; the absolute deadline for minor updates or withdrawal is December 14, 2026. Notifications are sent in mid-January 2027.
