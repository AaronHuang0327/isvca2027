Travel awards

Three awards help offset the cost of attending ISVCA 2027 in person: the ISVCA Biannual Congress Scientific Award, the TTS ISVCA Congress Scientific Award and the Nakos Travel Award. All three are administered by ISVCA, and all are applied for by email — not through the abstract submission form.

Application deadline November 30, 2026

ISVCA Biannual Congress Scientific Award

ISVCA Biannual Congress Scientific Awards are based on the scientific merit of abstracts received from members who are trainees, allied health professionals and/or residents of emerging economy countries. The awards, not exceeding $1,000/awardee, are intended to help offset expenses associated with attending the Congress and thus disbursement amounts may vary depending on location.

$1,000Maximum per awardee (USD)
5Maximum awards

Eligibility

To be eligible for an ISVCA Biannual Congress Scientific Award, applicants must:

  1. submit at least one abstract as a presenting author.
  2. be a trainee, Allied Health Professional or resident of an emerging economy country. Trainees must be within two (2) years of completion of their training and/or fellowship or provide proof of residency.
  3. be a member of ISVCA. If an applicant is not a member of The International Society for Vascularized Composite Allotransplantation, a membership application must be completed on the ISVCA website, www.tts.org/isvca, and accepted for the award application to be reviewed and considered. The membership application must be initiated prior to the Congress abstract submission closure date.
  4. for trainees, a supporting letter must be submitted as a part of the travel award application. This letter must be signed by the applicant's Training Program Director to verify the applicant's status as a trainee/young investigator. Candidates will not be considered for an award if the required letter is not sent.
  5. attend in-person the designated award presentation session scheduled during the Congress to receive the award. The applicant must be the presenting author.

Process for bestowing the award

  • The award aims to help cover the cost of travel and accommodation during the meeting. Recipients must attend the meeting in order to receive the award. If an awardee is selected for a scientific award but does not attend the meeting, the monies of the award will be forfeited.
  • Awardees will be selected from the pool of eligible applicants based upon the abstract's degree of excellence (top score), as determined by the Congress Scientific Program Committee.
  • Awards may not exceed a maximum amount of $1,000 USD per awardee. A maximum of five (5) awards will be provided.
  • Final selection of recipients will be approved by ISVCA.

TTS ISVCA Congress Scientific Award

Eligibility

To be eligible for a TTS ISVCA Congress Scientific Award, applicants must:

  1. submit at least one abstract as a presenting author.
  2. be a trainee, Allied Health Professional, or resident of an emerging economy country. Trainees must be within two (2) years of completion of their training and/or fellowship or provide proof of residency.
  3. be a member of TTS. If an applicant is not a member of The Transplantation Society, a membership application must be completed on the TTS website, www.tts.org, and accepted for the award application to be reviewed and considered. The membership application must be initiated prior to the Congress abstract submission closure date.
  4. for trainees, a supporting letter must be submitted as a part of the travel award application. This letter must be signed by the applicant's Training Program Director to verify the applicant's status as a trainee/young investigator. Candidates will not be considered for an award if the required letter is not sent.
  5. attend in-person the designated award presentation session scheduled during the Congress to receive the award. The applicant must be the presenting author.

Process for bestowing the award

  • Awardees will be selected from the pool of eligible applicants based upon the abstract's degree of excellence (top score), as determined by the Section Congress Scientific Program Committee.
  • Every effort will be made by TTS to award at least one scientific award to an applicant from each of the six TTS geographic regions (if applicable). The geographic regions are North America, Europe, Latin America, Asia, Middle East & Africa and Oceania.
  • Awards may not exceed a maximum amount of $1,000 USD per awardee. A maximum of five (5) awards will be provided per Congress.
  • Final selection of recipients will be approved by TTS.

Nakos Travel Award

ISVCA Biannual Congress Nakos Travel Awards are available for the generous support from the Nakos family. The awards, $1,000/awardee, are intended to help offset expenses associated with attending the Congress and thus disbursement amounts may vary depending on location.

No abstract required. Unlike the two Congress Scientific Awards, the Nakos Travel Award is open to eligible applicants who are not submitting an abstract. You still need to be an ISVCA member and to attend the award presentation session in person.

How to apply

Applications for all three awards are made by email. The abstract submission form does not collect award applications — submitting an abstract does not apply for an award.

Send your application to ISVCATravelAwards@gmail.com

Your email must include:

  • a short introduction for the use of the grant;
  • the applicant's CV; and
  • a supporting letter from their institution, if required (see eligibility above).
  • It is possible to apply for one, two, or three award categories in the same application. However, only one grant will be given to each applicant.
  • The application deadline is November 30, 2026, the same date as the abstract submission deadline.
  • Society membership must be initiated prior to the Congress abstract submission closure date.
  • Applications without the required supporting letter will not be considered.
  • For the two Congress Scientific Awards, the applicant must be the presenting author of the abstract.

The awards are administered by ISVCA, not by the ISVCA 2027 Organizing Committee. Please send applications and award enquiries to ISVCATravelAwards@gmail.com; the Organizing Committee cannot accept or forward applications. For questions about the Congress itself, contact program@isvca2027.org.

Notification & registration

Award notifications are sent in mid-January 2027, and in any case before the early-bird registration deadline of February 15, 2027, so that applicants who are not selected can still register at the early-bird rate.

If you applied for an ISVCA or TTS ISVCA Congress Scientific Award

Recipients receive complimentary registration, so please wait until award notifications are sent in mid-January 2027 before registering — recipients will be sent a dedicated registration link and will not be charged a registration fee.

There is time to do this: notifications are sent in mid-January, presenting authors must complete registration by February 1, 2027, and early-bird rates remain available until February 15, 2027.

If you applied for the Nakos Travel Award

The Nakos Travel Award does not include complimentary registration. Recipients register and pay in the normal way, and may use the grant towards the registration fee as well as travel and accommodation. You do not need to wait for the notification before registering.
