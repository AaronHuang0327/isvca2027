The 18th ISVCA Meeting comes to Taiwan

Held every two years, the ISVCA Meeting is the principal global forum for vascularized composite allotransplantation. In 2027 it comes to Taiwan for its 18th edition, convening surgeons, immunologists, scientists and allied health professionals across hand, face and other composite tissue transplantation.

The meeting is hosted by Linkou Chang Gung Memorial Hospital, through its Department of Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery and the Center for Reconstruction, Transplantation and Regeneration Research — a team with a long-standing program in reconstructive transplantation. Alongside the global status of the field, the program includes a dedicated session on VCA highlights in Asia.

Call for abstracts

We aim to create a broad and balanced program and, above all, we encourage you to reserve your best and most thoughtful work for this international meeting.

  1. Prepare your abstract

    Download the official Word template. Structure the body as Introduction, Methods, Results and Conclusions. Do not include the title, authors or affiliations in the document — review is blinded.

  2. Submit by November 30, 2026

    Abstracts must be in English. You may submit as many as you wish — one form per abstract. Every abstract is peer-reviewed for oral or poster presentation.

  3. Apply for a Congress Scientific Award

    Eligible applicants apply during submission by ticking the award option and uploading a supporting letter from their Training Program Director. Award criteria

  4. Register by February 1, 2027

    Notifications are sent mid-January. Only abstracts of presenting authors who have finalized registration and payment by February 1 will be scheduled.

Congress Scientific Awards

Two award streams help offset the cost of attending ISVCA 2027 in person. Application deadline: November 30, 2026.

ISVCA

ISVCA Congress Scientific Award

Up to USD 1,000 per awardee · maximum 5 awards

Helps cover the cost of travel and accommodation during the meeting. Applicants must be an ISVCA member, submit an abstract as presenting author, and be a trainee, AHP or resident of an emerging economy country. Final selection is approved by ISVCA.

TTS

TTS ISVCA Congress Scientific Award

Up to USD 1,000 per awardee · maximum 5 awards per Congress

Designed to help offset expenses incurred to attend the Congress in person, awarded on scientific merit. Applicants must be a TTS member and meet the same eligibility criteria. Final selection is approved by TTS.

Common conditions. Recipients must attend the meeting and attend the designated award presentation session to receive the award; if an awardee is selected but does not attend, the monies of the award will be forfeited. A supporting letter signed by the applicant's Training Program Director is required, and the applicant must be the presenting author.

Applying for an award? Recipients receive complimentary registration — please wait until award notifications in mid-January 2027 before registering. Recipients will be sent a dedicated registration link. Early-bird rates run until February 15, 2027.

Tick the award option when you submit your abstract. More specific application details for both ISVCA and TTS awards will be published shortly. Full eligibility & selection process →
