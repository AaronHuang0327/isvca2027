Call for Abstracts

Abstract submission guidelines

Everything you need to prepare and submit an abstract for the 18th Meeting of the International Society of Vascularized Composite Allotransplantation. Please read carefully — abstracts that do not follow these instructions may not be reviewed.

Deadline November 30, 2026, 23:59 US Eastern Time — 12:59, Dec 1 in Taiwan. No late-breaking submissions.

At a glance

Abstracts are submitted in English and peer-reviewed for oral or poster presentation. You may submit as many abstracts as you wish. The key limits are:

RequirementLimit
TitleMaximum 100 characters. No abbreviations in the title.
BodyMaximum 3,000 characters including spaces (roughly 400+ words), structured Introduction / Methods / Results / Conclusions.
FiguresUp to 2, as JPG / GIF / PNG (ideally 600 dpi, max 2 MB each). No tables in the body — upload tables as figures.
KeywordsExactly 4, chosen from the portal dropdown lists.
AbbreviationsAt most 3 nonstandard abbreviations, each defined at first use.
LanguageEnglish for both the abstract and the presentation.

Publication. Accepted abstracts are expected to be published online in an official meeting supplement; publication details, including the journal, will be confirmed by the organizing committee. Abstracts not accepted for presentation will not be published.

Audience & purpose

The meeting is designed for surgeons, immunologists, scientists and allied health professionals interested in the clinical and research aspects of vascularized composite allotransplantation. Material is presented through symposia, concurrent workshops, peer-reviewed oral and poster presentations, and small parallel workshops for in-depth discussion of clinical and basic-science topics.

The meeting aims to review the latest clinical and basic-science advances in VCA medicine and surgery; to examine recent developments in transplant biology, immunology and graft preservation; and to consider the broader social and policy issues in the field. Above all, we encourage you to reserve your best and most thoughtful work for this international meeting.

Structure & preparation

Abstracts must conform exactly to the official template. Download the MS Word template, insert your content under the four headings, and upload it through the submission form.

Do not include the title, author names or affiliations in the document. Doing so compromises the blinded review process and may result in automatic rejection.

The body is organised into four sections

Introduction
A short introduction indicating the rationale of the study.
Methods
A brief description of the pertinent methodological procedures.
Results
A summary of the results of the research.
Conclusions
A statement of the main conclusions.

Abbreviations should follow the Style Manual for Biological Journals. Use capitalised proprietary or product names only when absolutely necessary; otherwise use lowercase, chemically generic drug names. Submit figures with no surrounding white space, and make sure text stays readable when scaled down for print.

Review & changes

  • Abstracts are blinded for peer review. Be cautious with institutional references in the body — abstracts that identify authors or institutions will not be reviewed.
  • An abstract is eligible only if it has not been previously published as an article at the time of submission.
  • You may edit freely until the submission deadline. After the deadline, only minor administrative updates are allowed — author or institution spelling, disclosures, keywords, and questionnaire entries.
  • The absolute deadline for minor updates or withdrawals is December 14, 2026. No corrections, withdrawals or changes are accepted after this date.
  • Ensure all co-authors approve the submission, publication and potential presentation.
  • You will receive an email confirmation once your submission is finalised. The submitting author must complete their disclosure.

Ethics & integrity

Informed consent

Studies involving human subjects must conform to the Declaration of Helsinki and the Declaration of Istanbul, and meet all requirements governing informed consent in the country where the study was performed.

Use of animals

All studies involving animals must conform to the 1991 revision of the Guiding Principles in the Care and Use of Animals.

Plagiarism

Every abstract undergoes a plagiarism review. If plagiarism is found, all abstracts by that author are subject to immediate rejection, the submitting author's profile will be terminated, and any associated scholarship applications voided.

Employees of commercial interests

Employees of commercial interests are encouraged to submit, but abstracts should not include clinical patient-care recommendations. They should focus on biology, physiology or physics and the discovery process, and must not promote specific commercial products, treatments or diagnostics.

Clinical content validation

  • All clinical recommendations must rest on evidence accepted within the medical profession as adequate justification for patient-care indications and contraindications.
  • All research cited in support of a clinical recommendation must conform to generally accepted standards of experimental design, data collection and analysis.
  • Content must not promote recommendations, treatments or practice styles outside the definition of CME, or those known to carry risks outweighing clinical benefit, or known to be ineffective. Abstracts advocating unscientific diagnostic or therapeutic modalities will be rejected.

Submitting online

Abstracts are submitted through the online submission form. A few practical notes:

  • Complete one form per abstract. You may submit as many abstracts as you wish.
  • Prepare your files before you begin — the form is completed in a single session. You will need your abstract in MS Word (title, authors and affiliations removed), and optionally up to two figures.
  • Travel award applicants complete the award section of the same form and upload the supporting letter there.
  • You will receive an email confirmation once the submission is received.
  • To correct or withdraw an abstract after submitting, email program@isvca2027.org before December 14, 2026.

Embargo & publication

In compliance with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission's Regulation FD (Fair Disclosure), accepted abstracts are made public on the ISVCA website ahead of the meeting. The first public release is May 1, 2027.

Coverage of additional research presented at the meeting is embargoed until the time of presentation. Institutions, organisations and companies whose products may be affected by an abstract may issue a press release timed to the abstract's availability; however, anything beyond the content of the abstract itself — for example, discussion during a scientific presentation — is embargoed until the scientific sessions begin.

Violations may lead to withdrawal of the abstract and other appropriate measures. Journalists who break the embargo may have their press credentials revoked.
